This policy explains what information is collected through davidweis.com, why, and what choices you have.

Who we are

This website is operated by David Weis, a licensed real estate Broker Associate | REALTOR® in the State of California (DRE #02196688), affiliated with Compass (DRE #01527365). You can reach us atdavid@davidweis.com or 619.518.9246.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, email address, phone number, what you are interested in, the location you select and anything you write in the message field of the contact form.
  • Information collected automatically. Standard server and analytics data such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referring URL.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16, and we do not ask for Social Security numbers, financial account numbers or other sensitive identifiers through this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide real estate brokerage services.
  • To send you property information or market updates you asked for.
  • To improve how this site works.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and brokerage record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help operate this business, such as form handling, email, customer relationship management and analytics, and with Compass as our responsible broker where required for a transaction or by brokerage policy. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Your California privacy rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, to request deletion, to request correction, to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights. To make a request, email david@davidweis.com or call619.518.9246. We will verify your identity before acting on the request.

Cookies

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies; doing so may affect how parts of the site work.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law and brokerage record-keeping rules. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Third-party links

This site links to other websites, including compass.com. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy. The revised version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
